Bristol Community Events (BCE)

Bristol Community Events (BCE) exists to bring people together through unforgettable experiences across the city. From live music and cultural showcases to community meet-ups and creative gatherings, we help connect people with events that make Bristol feel alive. We believe events are more than just dates on a calendar — they’re moments that create memories, inspire creativity, and strengthen local communities.

Our Mission
Our mission is simple: to make discovering, booking, and enjoying events in Bristol easy, exciting, and accessible for everyone. We work to support local organisers, venues, and creators by giving them a platform to showcase their events — while giving residents and visitors a single place to explore what’s happening around them.
